## 0. Editorial method and precedence

- **[O]** = supported by the 39-page original guideline's section map and recovered text.
- **[A]** = supported by the current TA14 Academy Blueprint v0.2 as an educational architecture.
- **[P]** = proposed *new* TA14 governance requirement, requiring technical and institutional approval before it could become normative.
- **[U]** = unresolved; a controlling source or qualified technical approval is required.

If a requirement below carries more than one designation, those designations identify different claims rather than an unqualified equivalence. Exact engineering limits, manufacturer instructions, code obligations, hazard procedures, refrigerant handling requirements and final diagnostic stopping rules must come from applicable authoritative sources; nothing in this draft replaces them.

## 1. Purpose and boundaries

**[O]** Establish a sequential, measured, recorded, and traceable way to evaluate HVAC performance, determine what is supported, authorize bounded interventions and verify observed outcomes. The original proposal complements equipment-specific instructions and applicable codes/standards and does not itself define universal numerical performance thresholds (§§1–2).

**[P]** Add a separate consequence-authority check before an action changes physical reality. Evidence that supports a diagnosis does not by itself establish legal authority, customer permission, qualified capacity or standing to perform a particular action. An admissible execution determination must be bounded to the named consequence and the time of decision.

**[U]** The hierarchy and exact scope of every new TA14 criterion must be resolved before any normative use or standard-development request.

## 2. Original seven-stage service process — RETAIN ORDER

The following seven names reproduce the original guideline's §5.2 ordered stages. The Academy's nine teaching anchors should **not** be substituted for them without a formally identified revision.

| Original stage | Original purpose [O] | Proposed refinement [P] | Academy reference [A] |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Baseline Establishment** | Preserve contemporaneous measured system state and operating context, without reconstruction. | Explicitly distinguish non-invasive **Part A** from conditional, separately authorized refrigerant-side **Part B**. Protect the source/raw record across both phases. | Sequence, Non-Invasive Evidence, Original-State HPR |
| **2. Performance Evaluation** | Gather measured observations before declaring condition or repair. | Record source, time, instrument, measurement point, configuration, uncertainty and whether a statement is observed, computed or inferred. | Non-Invasive Evidence, HPR |
| **3. Threshold Determination** | Decide from non-intrusive data whether further diagnostic or intrusive progression is justified. | Add a referenced, versioned **NIRET** decision *for refrigerant-side entry*, separate from authorization to adjust, charge or repair. Record why a threshold is or is not met. | NIRET; conditional Refrigerant-Side Evidence |
| **4. Diagnostic Classification** | Make an evidence-supported determination or preserve an indeterminate state and continue justified investigation. | Add an attributable Declared Diagnostic Determination naming the supported condition, unresolved alternatives, evidence provenance and uncertainties. Keep **diagnostic termination** a distinct controlled decision pending the exact standard. | Declared Diagnostic Determination |
| **5. Intervention Authorization** | Scope and record a specific justified intervention *before* execution. | Examine **Admissible Evidence / Applicable Authority / Established Standing** for the named action; capture Actor, Capacity, Standing Basis and Authority Relationship. Record ALLOW / HOLD / DENY / ESCALATE, without silently equating any of them to commit. | Evidence-Based Intervention / governance boundary |
| **6. Intervention Execution** | Perform only the authorized action and preserve what was done. | Require an independent, affirmative bounded commit step where applicable and revalidate changed conditions before execution. No permission for one action automatically grants permission for another. | Evidence-Based Intervention |
| **7. Post-Intervention Verification** | Produce new post-work observations; compare to baseline with recorded limits; establish whether claimed correction is supported. | Preserve original and post records independently. Check comparability, show adjusted analyses **alongside raw** if justified, and limit outcome declaration to evidence. The proposed seven-label outcome taxonomy requires its own definitions and review. | HVAC Post-Performance Record; Proof of Performance |

**Critical interpretive boundary:** The original seven stages are a process structure. The nine Academy teaching anchors are a more granular presentation. The mapping here is explanatory and does **not** establish an officially amended seven-stage standard.

## 3. Governing record and decision fields — proposed extension [P]

For each proposed consequential action, the future implementation should be able to produce a reviewable record containing:

- Equipment/system identity, operating context, complaint, asset and location; exact observations, source, units, measurement points, time, instruments and raw state.
- Original non-intrusive baseline and time-separated contextual checks when specified by the controlling field standard; missing data and limitations recorded without backfilling.
- The particular evidence question, threshold authority/version, threshold disposition and why additional access is necessary—or why it is not.
- The separate named action: *observe refrigerant side*, *adjust refrigerant*, *replace part*, *change control setting*, *close service call*, etc. Do not merge these into one general permission.
- Diagnostic classification/DDD, evidence relied on, conflicting evidence, uncertainty, what remains unsupported, and any separate examination-stop determination.
- Responsible actor, capacity, standing basis, authority relationship, site/customer permission, relevant qualifications, safety and legal constraints, exact scope and validity window.
- ALLOW/HOLD/DENY/ESCALATE with rationale, any review or escalation path, any authorized commit and actual execution, the result, and a tamper-evident or otherwise reviewable history **only to the extent technically implemented and tested**.
- Distinct post-work observations, comparability limitations, raw and adjusted records if applicable, supported classification and unresolved conditions.

This field set is a **proposed authoring and testing target**, not a representation that the deployed TA14 implementation already captures or enforces every field.

## 4. Consequence dispositions — proposed extension [P]

**ALLOW:** Necessary applicable evidence, authority and standing support the specific proposed action now; this is **not** automatic commit or execution.

**HOLD:** Necessary support is missing, incomplete, conflicting, stale or not presently established. Do not take the governed consequential step until its prerequisites are met and re-examined.

**DENY:** The proposed consequence is disallowed on the established conditions. Denial of *refrigerant entry*, for example, does **not** automatically end the full service call; a different action requires its own examination.

**ESCALATE:** Local determination or execution is inappropriate without resolving a qualified authority, standing, capability, hazard or governance conflict.

**[O/A/P distinction]** The old original proposal describes threshold qualification, indeterminate conditions and bounded intervention. The later four-disposition model appears in TA14 architecture and P-01 simulator originating requirements. This section introduces it explicitly as a **candidate future normative extension**, not a quote from the original seven-stage submission.

## 5. NIRET and safe investigation — controlling criteria unresolved [U]

NIRET (Non-Invasive Refrigerant Entry Threshold) is an evidence necessity boundary for refrigerant-side observation. A threshold decision does not by itself authorize entry, service-port connection, recovery, charging, adjustment, repair, or any unrelated intervention.

Do **not** infer a universal minimum number of readings, a numerical pressure threshold, or an automatic refrigerant-defect diagnosis. The Academy's **current** governing field criteria, applicable site and safety rules, and separate actor authorization must be traced to their authenticated source/version. Earlier P-01 simulator source packages show example threshold logic but are **not** substitutes for a universal current Academy field rule.

Where an immediate lawful protective act is essential for safety, safety takes priority over perfect documentation. Preserve surviving evidence, identify which measurements are now impossible, and record exactly what action and scope were necessary. **The exact Academy emergency/termination exception is unresolved and must not be invented.**

## 6. Declared Diagnostic Determination versus diagnostic termination [A/P/U]

A DDD is an attributable explanation of what existing records support, what they do not support, and the uncertainty that remains. It is not an authorization to perform a repair.

The decision to **terminate diagnostic examination** is conceptually distinct from (i) declaring a supported diagnosis, (ii) deciding not to enter the refrigerant circuit, (iii) denying a particular proposed consequence, and (iv) closing the service call. **[U]** Do not prescribe exact permitted termination conditions until the Academy's current controlling wording is retrieved and technically approved.

## 7. Outcome classification and comparison — proposed extension [P]

Maintain distinct before-and-after raw data, with collection methods and operating context. Only compare directly where reasonably comparable, and retain raw and justified adjustments separately. Avoid converting one favorable post-work reading into a verified success claim.

Potential TA14 outcome classes requiring exact definitions, evidence requirements and independent validation: **IMPROVED, RESTORED, UNCHANGED, DEGRADED, PARTIALLY RESOLVED, UNRESOLVED, INDETERMINATE**. This taxonomy is **not asserted as verbatim text of the 39-page proposal**.

## 8. Evidence, safety and execution release tests

**Proposed red-team cases for a later engineering test plan [P]:**

1. Equipment measurements appear plausible, but the sample timestamp is stale before commit: does action remain blocked pending revalidation?
2. Airflow evidence conflicts with a proposed refrigerant fault: is refrigerant entry held and the contradiction recorded?
3. NIRET evidence is sufficient, but actor/site permission is missing: is entry prevented even when a technician is capable?
4. A work order authorizes observation, but software or an operator attempts refrigerant adjustment: is scope expansion denied/held?
5. A prior authorization expires between diagnosis and proposed execution: does a new examination occur before physical consequence?
6. Technician stops further refrigerant diagnostics, but other non-invasive service activity remains justified: does system avoid erroneously terminating the complete service call?
7. Raw pre/post records are noncomparable because loading conditions changed: is the outcome labeled indeterminate or otherwise narrowly supported?
8. A simulated HOLD exists in a dashboard, but the physical write path still executes: must label the architecture **advisory**, not enforced execution governance, on that path.

These are proposed **acceptance tests**, not tests currently passed. Do not assert operational enforcement, immutability or verified outcomes without corresponding implementation evidence.
