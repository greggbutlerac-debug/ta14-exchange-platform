**Examination performed:** Full-file machine-readable text extraction of all 39 pages; section/paragraph comparison against TA14 Academy HVAC Blueprint v0.2. ## Seven mandatory stages, verbatim titles (source §5.2)

1. Baseline Establishment
2. Performance Evaluation
3. Threshold Determination
4. Diagnostic Classification
5. Intervention Authorization
6. Intervention Execution
7. Post-Intervention Verification

The original explicitly prohibits omission, consolidation or reordering. Its seven stages must not be quoted as the Academy's nine. The modern Academy chain expresses: Sequence → Non-Invasive Evidence → NIRET → Refrigerant-Side Evidence (if justified and separately authorized) → Original-State HVAC Performance Record → Declared Diagnostic Determination → Evidence-Based Intervention → HVAC Post-Performance Record → Proof of Performance. The **seven-stage proposal and nine-stage teaching/authority chain are not mechanically interchangeable**. A revised proposal must explicitly identify its normative version and supply an authored change-control rationale if stage definitions change.

## Material reconciliation findings

**F-01 — Baseline vs intrusive evidence (HIGH).** Original §5.4 requires baseline prior to diagnostic determination/intervention, §5.6 requires threshold before system entry, component-level inspection, refrigerant access or corrective intervention. Original §7.4 prohibits such entry for the purpose of *establishing threshold qualification*. The Academy's original-state HPR may include separately authorized refrigerant-side Part B **after** NIRET. Reconcile the baseline as a phased record, specifying exactly what is non-intrusive Part A, what conditional Part B is, and what is immutable original-state evidence. Without a formal revision, avoid claiming the old seven-stage order already encoded Part A / NIRET / Part B.

**F-02 — Threshold vs NIRET (HIGH).** Original §7 speaks more broadly about intrusion, system entry and disassembly; it never specifies the later NIRET nomenclature or exact contemporary Academy gate conditions. §2.6 expressly delegates performance values to applicable standards, design and conditions. Neither generic threshold qualification nor the older two-reading simulator scenario may be treated as the full current Academy NIRET standard.

**F-03 — Diagnosis vs termination (HIGH).** §8.4 accepts an indeterminate classification and further non-intrusive evaluation. The original document does not establish the **exact later Academy diagnostic-termination wording**. A DDD, service-call closure and denial of a particular invasive consequence must not be merged into one decision. Locate or authoritatively resolve the current field rule before modifying normative Academy language.

**F-04 — Authorization vs standing, binding and commit (HIGH).** §§9.3–9.7 require explicit intervention authorization tied to measured evidence and a proposed bounded act. These sections do **not** separately define the modern TA14 standing anchors (Actor / Capacity / Standing Basis / Authority Relationship), nor ALLOW vs Commit or continuous revalidation. A subsequent governance revision could add these, but must distinguish newly introduced requirements from content already sent to NIST in 2026.

**F-05 — Outcome verification vs seven-state classification (MEDIUM).** §§10.4–10.8 recognize comparability limits and prohibit claiming verified corrective completion without sufficient post-work proof. The original does **not** express the later seven TA14 outcome labels as its own normative taxonomy; if adopted in a new version, add definitions and evidence tests, keeping raw/adjusted measurements separate.

**F-06 — Compliance claims and exceptions (HIGH).** The 39-page proposal frequently states that failure to follow its sequence makes a process 'non-compliant.' This is compliance **with the proposed guideline only**, not an ASHRAE code, certification or regulatory consequence. The revised text must carefully address safety-critical protective action, inaccessible records and legitimate bounded deferral rather than inadvertently teaching that safety requires waiting for perfect baseline records.

**F-07 — Original submission vs updated proposal (HIGH).** Source has ASHRAE-oriented proposal discussion; preserve this as a request for technical consideration. It is **not** an ASHRAE standard, and NIST receipt or ASHRAE correspondence is **not** validation or adoption. Any expanded guideline must receive a new version number, revision table, named contributors and explicit 'proposed' status.

**F-08 — Learning architecture vs service normative standard (MEDIUM).** Academy images, narrated lessons, and assessment domains are pedagogy, not automatically field requirements. Retain the existing Academy visuals the owner prefers; do not graft the other eight-image draft into the primary page or confuse 'eight lessons' with 'nine stages' or 'seven guideline stages.'
