## Release/standards boundary

- **No ASHRAE, NIST or other institution endorsement, adoption or validation.**
- ASHRAE TC 7.5 has received an introductory technical examination; no response or formal adoption has been established.
- A service-process guideline is not interchangeable with a software execution-governance proof, an Academy training page, or a certification.
- Do not silently edit the source PDF and call the edited artifact the same version.
- Do not send a duplicate to TC 7.5 while its 2026-10-17 outreach remains pending without a deliberate follow-up decision.

## Required work before any external update

1. Obtain and review **every section of the complete 39-page source**, preserving source wording, pagination, scope and normative distinctions.
2. Locate exact current controlling Academy NIRET and diagnostic-termination field rules, or record that they remain missing; never elevate P-01 simulator conditions to Academy-wide field requirements.
3. Prepare a **new versioned proposal** and change log, distinguishing the original 2026-09-30 delivered version from new post-submission architecture.
4. Improve the existing Academy teaching page only after an image-by-image content/technical review. Reuse its preferred images and narration; avoid a competing second standard or duplicate production showroom.
5. Have a qualified HVAC technical examiner review measurements, electrical and refrigerant safety, scope, and outcomes. Record independently supported findings; keep conceptual examples clearly identified.
6. Confirm target committee/process and the user's approval before sending or publishing a new version.

**Disposition: HOLD — consolidation work authorized; revised technical standard, visuals and any external transmission not yet approved.**
