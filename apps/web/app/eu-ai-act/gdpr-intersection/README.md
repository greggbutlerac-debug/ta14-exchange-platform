# GDPR × EU AI Act governed intersection

This route preserves GDPR personal-data governance and EU AI Act AI-risk governance as independent determination boundaries.

## Invariants

- A favorable GDPR evidence state must never manufacture a favorable EU AI Act state.
- A favorable EU AI Act evidence state must never manufacture a favorable GDPR state.
- Missing, stale, contradicted, or unresolved evidence fails closed to an incomplete or review-required state.
- Material changes to purpose, personal-data scope, controller/processor role, AI-system role/classification, deployment context, authority, oversight, or evidence trigger revalidation.
- Shared evidence may support propositions in both boundaries but does not collapse the boundaries.
- Evidence of a control does not itself create authority to execute.
- TA-14 outputs are bounded governance records, not legal advice or statutory certification.

## Integration target

Primary route: `/eu-ai-act/gdpr-intersection`

The EU AI Act world should expose this route as a distinct cross-regulation pathway rather than presenting GDPR as an EU AI Act requirement.
