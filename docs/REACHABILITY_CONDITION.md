# TA-14 Reachability Condition

TA-14 Admissible Continuity Architecture v1.3 formalizes **Reachability** as a cross-cutting survival condition of continuity.

> **Canonical test:** Did changed reality remain capable of changing what happened next before consequence became effectively irreversible?

Reachability requires that a material change in source reality remain capable of reaching and altering the consequence-bearing route in time to support reassessment, narrowing, correction, escalation, refusal, revocation, binding, commit, or execution control.

Reachability is not a new canonical TA-14 chain stage and does not expand the AC-8 Continuity Survival Engine into AC-9. It is expressed through AC-3 Temporal Continuity, AC-7 Challenge / Refusal Continuity, and the Runtime Continuity Gate.

A historically continuous record is not necessarily operationally continuous if changed reality can no longer reach the consequence-bearing path.
