## Material limitations preserved

### X03 — Revocation reachability

HSG structurally distinguishes current authority, prior historical authority, in-flight execution, and actual effectuation. Revocation does not retroactively erase an already-effectuated consequence. Where an external or remote system cannot receive or effectuate revocation before consequence, interruption cannot be presumed. Universal revocation reachability is therefore **not established**.

### X08 — Observability and unknown descendants

HSG does not manufacture knowledge of hidden actors, undeclared dependencies, concealed control relationships, unknown delegation descendants, or execution paths outside the observable or authorized governance surface. Revocation of known and reachable lineage does not establish universal revocation of unknown or unreachable descendants. Complete descendant revocation remains **unestablished where complete lineage or reachability is unestablished**.

These limitations are part of the finding and may not be removed when the determination is quoted, summarized, published, or relied upon.
