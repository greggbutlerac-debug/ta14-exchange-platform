## 1. Purpose

These Terms establish the public operating boundary for registration, evidence submission, evidence preservation, governed review, findings, publication, withdrawal, versioning, intellectual-property treatment, institutional succession, and institutional recordkeeping within the TA14 AI Governance Exchange.

These Terms are **designed to be understandable by a third party who has not participated in private discussions with TA14 Authority or a registrant.**

That design objective is not itself proof that independent readability has been established.

### Independent-read status

As of Version 1.1:

**INDEPENDENT-READ VALIDATION: NOT YET COMPLETED**

TA14 Authority will not represent these Terms as independently validated until an actual independent-read event has occurred and the resulting validation record has been preserved.

Registration is not conditioned on completion of that validation.

No private conversation, informal explanation, direct message, email, meeting, or unpublished understanding is intended to supply a material rule absent from these Terms.

Where these Terms fail to state a material condition clearly enough for an outside party to understand it, the omission should be treated as a defect to be corrected rather than silently filled by presumed institutional intent.

# PART I — REGISTRATION

## 2. What Governance Entity Registration Means

Governance Entity Registration establishes an attributable institutional record for an AI governance entity.

A registration may preserve, as applicable:

- governance name;
- short name;
- governance category;
- steward, founder, claimant, organization, or other attributable party;
- version;
- establishment or effective date;
- jurisdiction;
- regulatory or operational scope;
- bounded claims;
- explicit non-claims;
- declared limitations;
- implementation state;
- public references;
- repository references;
- publications;
- evidence references;
- ownership or stewardship declarations;
- record visibility;
- version lineage;
- subsequent governed activity.

Registration establishes what the registrant declares about the governance entity at the time of registration.

Registration creates an attributable baseline.

Registration is not itself a substantive finding about the correctness of that baseline.

---

## 3. What Registration Does Not Mean

Registration does **not** mean that TA14 Authority has:

- certified the governance entity;
- approved the governance entity;
- endorsed the governance entity;
- validated its architecture;
- verified every submitted claim;
- established regulatory compliance;
- established legal compliance;
- established technical correctness;
- established cybersecurity adequacy;
- established production readiness;
- established market fitness;
- established ownership as a matter of law;
- established patentability;
- established non-infringement;
- determined that a particular execution is admissible;
- determined that the governance entity is superior to another architecture;
- granted the registrant authority it did not otherwise possess.

A registration identifier is an institutional identity and chronology mechanism.

It is not a quality mark.

---

## 4. Registration Before Artifact Registration

An execution artifact, evidence artifact, governed demonstration artifact, receipt, conformance record, or related governed record may be attributed to an AI governance entity only after the relevant governance entity has completed Governance Entity Registration.

The governing sequence is:

**Governance Entity Registration → Attributable Governance Identity → Artifact Registration**

The existence of a registered artifact does not convert the underlying governance registration into certification or approval.

---

## 5. Registrant Declaration

The registrant is responsible for the declarations it submits.

TA14 Authority preserves those declarations as attributable statements of the registrant unless TA14 Authority separately identifies a statement as independently verified, independently reproduced, independently sourced, or otherwise supported through a distinct review process.

A registrant must not knowingly present:

- fabricated evidence;
- materially false attribution;
- deliberately altered evidence represented as original;
- false ownership declarations;
- knowingly false version statements;
- falsified chronology;
- fabricated third-party endorsements;
- fabricated regulatory status;
- fabricated certification;
- fabricated independent verification.

Discovery of a material integrity issue may result in a record being:

- held;
- flagged;
- disputed;
- corrected;
- superseded;
- withdrawn from current reliance;
- referred for further review.

Historical events will not be silently erased merely because a later dispute arises.

# PART II — CLOSED SYSTEMS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

## 6. Closed Implementations May Remain Closed

Governance Entity Registration does not require a registrant to disclose source code, proprietary models, confidential internals, private infrastructure, trade secrets, private datasets, private credentials, private customer materials, or other non-public implementation details merely because the governance entity is registered.

A closed implementation may remain closed.

A registrant may instead submit an evidence surface appropriate to the bounded claim being reviewed.

That evidence surface may include:

- outputs;
- receipts;
- signed records;
- hashes;
- logs;
- conformance results;
- public specifications;
- architecture descriptions;
- demonstrations;
- evaluation packets;
- independent reports;
- screenshots;
- preserved states;
- version manifests;
- execution records;
- outcome records;
- other bounded evidence objects.

TA14 Authority will not imply that access to submitted outputs constitutes access to unsubmitted internals.

---

## 7. Ownership

Registration does not transfer ownership of the registrant's intellectual property to TA14 Authority.

Unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise:

- the registrant retains ownership of its architecture;
- the registrant retains ownership of its submitted original works;
- the registrant retains ownership of its trademarks and names;
- the registrant retains ownership of its proprietary implementation;
- TA14 Authority retains ownership of TA14 Authority-authored findings, schemas, review instruments, institutional publications, and TA14-created records.

Third-party materials remain subject to the rights of their respective owners.

---

## 8. No Implied Implementation License

Submission of evidence does not, by itself, grant TA14 Authority a license to:

- commercialize the registrant's architecture;
- reproduce a closed implementation;
- create derivative implementations;
- redistribute proprietary source code;
- reverse engineer proprietary systems;
- sublicense proprietary technology;
- use submitted confidential materials for unrelated commercial development.

Any broader license must be separately and expressly stated in writing.

# PART III — EVIDENCE

## 9. Evidence Is Admitted, Not Assumed

TA14 Authority evaluates admitted evidence.

The existence of a statement inside a registration does not automatically make the statement independently established.

The existence of a runtime output does not automatically establish the surrounding chronology that produced that output.

The existence of a hash does not automatically prove that the underlying information was truthful when originally recorded.

The existence of a preserved record does not automatically establish independent attestation.

TA14 Authority will distinguish those properties where material.

---

## 10. Evidence Provenance States

Material evidence used in a finding should be attributed using one or more explicit provenance states.

### REGISTRANT-PRODUCED

The evidence was generated, supplied, maintained, or represented by the governance entity or registrant being evaluated.

### TA14-PRODUCED

The evidence or record was generated directly by TA14 Authority through its own institutional process.

### INDEPENDENTLY PRODUCED

The evidence originated from a party materially independent of both the registrant and TA14 Authority for the relevant evidentiary purpose.

### INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED

A material behavior, result, or claim was reproduced by an independent party under conditions sufficient to support the stated finding.

### PUBLIC-SOURCE

The evidence was obtained from an attributable public source.

### CROSS-PARTY

The record depends on evidence or receipts preserved by more than one party.

### NOT INDEPENDENTLY ESTABLISHED

The material proposition may be represented in submitted evidence, but the review record does not independently establish it.

### NOT REPORTED

The relevant provenance, mechanism, or source was not reported in the admitted record.

### NOT SUBMITTED

The evidence object required to establish the proposition was not submitted.

### NOT PRESERVED

The relevant record was not contemporaneously preserved.

### OUTSIDE REVIEW SCOPE

The matter was not evaluated within the bounded scope of the proceeding.

These states are evidentiary descriptions.

They are not automatically positive or negative judgments.

---

## 11. No Favorable Meaning From a Blank

Where provenance, preservation, independence, or mechanism attribution is material, a blank field must not be treated as neutral.

A missing state should be represented affirmatively where possible.

Examples include:

**NOT REPORTED**

**NOT SUBMITTED**

**NOT PRESERVED**

**NOT INDEPENDENTLY ESTABLISHED**

**OUTSIDE REVIEW SCOPE**

Silence must not be used to imply the strongest available interpretation.

---

# PART IV — PORTABLE FINDINGS

## 12. Material Qualifications Must Travel With the Finding

A material limitation that changes the meaning of a finding must remain attached to the finding it qualifies.

TA14 Authority should not publish a favorable headline while placing a material limiting condition somewhere that can easily be separated from it.

For example, TA14 Authority should avoid a portable result such as:

> SUPPORTED

when the evidence actually supports only:

> **SUPPORTED — registrant-produced evidence; independently reproducible under the reviewed test conditions; execution-path coverage not independently established.**

Likewise:

> **RUNTIME BEHAVIOR DEMONSTRATED — full surrounding institutional chronology not independently established.**

The qualification is part of the finding.

It is not optional explanatory metadata.

---

## 13. Finding Provenance

A material TA14 finding should identify, where applicable:

- what the registrant declared;
- what evidence the registrant produced;
- what TA14 Authority produced;
- what an independent party produced;
- what another party reproduced;
- what evidence was not preserved;
- what evidence was not submitted;
- what propositions were not independently established;
- what remained outside scope.

TA14 Authority will not describe institutional position alone as independent verification.

---

# PART V — FINDINGS AND REVIEW

## 14. Bounded Findings

TA14 findings are limited to:

- the registered entity;
- the identified version;
- the declared jurisdiction;
- the bounded claim;
- the admitted evidence;
- the observed conditions;
- the review scope;
- the relevant execution or demonstration route;
- the review date or preserved chronology.

A finding does not silently extend beyond those boundaries.

---

## 15. Possible Findings

Depending on the applicable review pathway, findings may include states such as:

- SUPPORTED;
- CONDITIONALLY SUPPORTED;
- PARTIALLY SUPPORTED;
- NOT FULLY ESTABLISHED;
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE;
- HELD;
- DENIED;
- ESCALATED;
- REFUSED;
- OUTSIDE SCOPE;
- NOT REVIEWABLE.

The exact meaning of a finding must be defined by the instrument or record that uses it.

TA14 Authority will not convert an evidence limitation into a broader failure than the admitted record supports.

TA14 Authority will also not convert a limited success into a broader PASS than the admitted record supports.

---

## 16. No Predetermined Outcome

Participation in a TA14 governed review does not require the registrant to produce a PASS.

A valid outcome may reveal:

- demonstrated behavior;
- failed behavior;
- refusal;
- escalation;
- incomplete evidence;
- a continuity break;
- a preservation failure;
- a missing artifact;
- a bounded success;
- a bounded limitation;
- a condition not tested.

A finding that accurately identifies a limitation is not treated as a defective finding merely because it is less favorable than the registrant hoped.

---

# PART VI — EVIDENCE PRESERVATION

## 17. No Retrospective Evidence Manufacturing

TA14 Authority does not require a registrant to manufacture evidence after the fact merely to complete an evidentiary chain.

Where a relevant contemporaneous record did not exist or was not preserved, the appropriate state may be:

**NOT PRESERVED / EVIDENTIARY LIMITATION**

A later reconstruction may be submitted as a reconstruction only if it is clearly identified as such.

It must not be silently represented as contemporaneous evidence.

---

## 18. Frozen Records

Where a demonstration, version, case, or artifact has been formally frozen, later implementation changes must not silently alter the earlier record.

The appropriate lineage is:

**Frozen Version → Preserved Finding → Later Version**

not:

**Frozen Version → Later Changes → Rewritten Earlier Finding**

Later engineering development may respond to a prior finding without modifying what the earlier evidence established.

---

# PART VII — PUBLICATION, CUSTODY, AND RETENTION

## 19. Public and Controlled Materials

Each submitted evidence object should have an explicit visibility state.

Possible states may include:

- PUBLIC;
- CONTROLLED;
- PRIVATE;
- REGISTRY-ONLY;
- REVIEW-ONLY;
- WITHHELD FROM PUBLICATION.

TA14 Authority should not convert a controlled or private artifact into a public artifact merely because the artifact influenced a public finding.

A public finding may describe the evidentiary effect of a controlled artifact without publishing the controlled artifact itself, subject to the applicable review boundary.

---

## 20. TA14 Publication Rights

Unless a separate instrument provides otherwise, submission to a governed TA14 pathway authorizes TA14 Authority to preserve the existence, identity, chronology, status, and evidentiary role of the submission as necessary to maintain the institutional record.

Public republication of the submitted artifact itself depends on its declared visibility and applicable rights.

TA14 Authority may publish its own finding about admitted evidence without acquiring ownership of the underlying evidence.

---

## 21. Quotations and Excerpts

Where publication rights permit, TA14 Authority may use limited quotations, excerpts, metadata, descriptions, hashes, identifiers, or evidentiary references reasonably necessary to explain an institutional finding.

TA14 Authority should not use a fragment in a way that materially reverses or obscures the meaning of the submitted record.

---

## 22. Chronology and Evidence Files Are Different Objects

TA14 Authority distinguishes between:

**institutional chronology**

and

**submitted evidence files.**

Institutional chronology may include:

- the fact that registration occurred;
- registration identifier;
- entity identity;
- version;
- registration date;
- case identifiers;
- artifact identifiers;
- evidence-admission events;
- findings;
- correction events;
- withdrawal events;
- supersession events;
- participant responses;
- provenance classifications;
- hashes or integrity references where appropriate.

Submitted evidence files may include:

- documents;
- screenshots;
- logs;
- receipts;
- datasets;
- reports;
- test outputs;
- controlled artifacts;
- private artifacts;
- other uploaded source material.

The preservation of institutional chronology does not automatically require indefinite retention or public republication of every underlying evidence file.

---

## 23. Evidence-File Retention After Withdrawal

Withdrawal does not by itself erase institutional chronology.

Evidence-file custody is evaluated separately.

For evidence classified as PUBLIC and already published as part of the institutional record, the published historical copy may remain preserved unless a separate legal, rights, security, or correction basis requires another treatment.

For CONTROLLED, PRIVATE, REVIEW-ONLY, REGISTRY-ONLY, or WITHHELD FROM PUBLICATION evidence, withdrawal may include a request concerning continued custody of the underlying evidence file.

TA14 Authority should distinguish among:

- preservation of chronology;
- preservation of metadata;
- preservation of integrity references or hashes;
- continued custody of the underlying evidence file;
- continued public availability of the underlying evidence file.

Where the underlying evidence file is no longer retained, the institutional record may state:

**EVIDENCE FILE NO LONGER HELD — HISTORICAL CHRONOLOGY PRESERVED**

TA14 Authority will not imply that it continues to possess an evidence file after that file is no longer held.

Retention may remain subject to applicable law, security requirements, dispute preservation, active review obligations, backup cycles, or separately agreed terms.

---

# PART VIII — WITHDRAWAL

## 24. Withdrawal of Participation

A registrant may request withdrawal from future voluntary participation in a TA14 process.

Withdrawal from future participation does not automatically erase historical institutional events that already occurred.

Examples include:

- a registration that was issued;
- a case that was opened;
- evidence that was formally admitted;
- a finding that was published;
- a participant response that was preserved;
- an artifact that was registered;
- a dispute that entered the record.

TA14 Authority distinguishes:

**withdrawal from continuing participation**

from

**erasure of historical chronology.**

They are not the same operation.

---

## 25. Withdrawal of a Registration

Where a registration is withdrawn from current active use, TA14 Authority may mark the record with an appropriate state such as:

**WITHDRAWN**

**INACTIVE**

**SUPERSEDED**

**RETIRED**

The historical fact that the record existed may remain preserved.

TA14 Authority should not imply that a withdrawn registration remains currently active.

---

## 26. Withdrawal Does Not Rewrite Findings

A registrant's later withdrawal does not automatically nullify or erase a finding that was validly produced from the admitted record at the time.

Likewise, TA14 Authority should not continue to present a historical finding as though it automatically applies to materially changed later versions.

---

# PART IX — CORRECTIONS, DISPUTES, AND RESPONSES

## 27. Factual Corrections

A registrant or other affected party may request correction of an objective record error.

Examples include:

- incorrect version;
- incorrect identifier;
- incorrect timestamp;
- incorrect artifact name;
- transcription error;
- incorrect attribution;
- incorrect source reference.

Correction of a factual record error does not require the participant to agree with TA14 Authority's substantive finding.

---

## 28. Participant Responses

A participant may disagree with a TA14 finding.

That disagreement should be preserved separately rather than silently rewriting the TA14 finding.

The institutional model is:

**TA14 Finding → Participant Response**

not:

**TA14 Finding + Participant Disagreement → Compromised Single Voice**

Separate voices remain separately attributable.

---

## 29. Reviews & Responses

TA14 Authority may preserve governed pathways including:

- Participant Reviews;
- Participant Responses;
- Independent Reviews;
- Evidence Challenges;
- Factual Correction Requests;
- Technical Comments;
- Replication Requests;
- Demonstration Requests;
- External Publication References.

These records should be:

- attributable;
- dated;
- linked to the relevant governed record;
- preserved as separate voices;
- protected against silent rewriting of prior findings.

---

# PART X — GOVERNANCE VERSIONING

## 30. Governance Version Lineage

A later governance version should be distinguishable from an earlier version.

The later version may inherit historical lineage.

It does not automatically inherit:

- a PASS;
- a favorable finding;
- evidence sufficiency;
- production readiness;
- independent verification;
- a prior demonstrated outcome.

Each material version must stand on the evidence applicable to that version.

---

## 31. Findings Do Not Automatically Transfer Across Governance Versions

A finding concerning Version 1.0 does not automatically establish the same proposition for Version 2.0.

Likewise, a limitation identified in Version 1.0 must not automatically be attributed to Version 2.0 without evidence that the limitation remains present.

TA14 Authority preserves lineage without collapsing versions.

---

## 32. Registrations Preserve the Terms Version Accepted

Every governed registration or material submission should preserve the version of these Terms applicable when the relevant acceptance occurred.

Example:

**Terms Accepted: TA14-RET-001 Version 1.1**

A later version of these Terms does not silently replace the historical terms-version reference attached to an earlier registration or frozen event.

---

## 33. Later Terms Apply Prospectively

Materially changed later Terms apply prospectively.

A later Terms version does not automatically rewrite:

- an earlier registration;
- an earlier evidence admission;
- an earlier frozen demonstration;
- an earlier finding;
- an earlier withdrawal event;
- rights or boundaries already preserved in the historical institutional record.

Where continued participation in a new pathway requires acceptance of updated Terms, TA14 Authority should obtain and preserve that acceptance before the new governed activity begins.

The resulting record may therefore contain:

**Original Registration — Terms Version 1.1**

followed later by:

**New Governed Activity — Terms Version 1.2 Accepted**

This preserves both chronology and prospective institutional change.

---

## 34. No Silent Migration

TA14 Authority must not silently migrate a historical record from one Terms version to another.

If a legal, regulatory, security, or operational requirement makes a prospective change necessary, the changed rule and its effective boundary should be identified explicitly.

A new Terms version does not convert a historical event into an event that occurred under rules that did not yet exist.

# PART XIII — WHAT ATTESTS TA14 AUTHORITY?

## 37. Institutional Position Is Not Independence

TA14 Authority does not claim that a record becomes independently verified merely because TA14 Authority produced, stored, signed, hashed, published, or preserved it.

Integrity and independence are separate properties.

An internally preserved TA14 record may provide:

- chronology;
- attributable authorship;
- integrity evidence;
- lineage;
- review history;
- institutional accountability.

Those properties do not automatically establish external independence.

---

## 38. The Independence Termination Point Must Be Visible

Where a TA14 finding depends on evidence whose independence terminates at a particular point, that termination point should be visible.

TA14 Authority should distinguish among:

- registrant assertion;
- TA14 observation;
- TA14-generated record;
- third-party source;
- independent reproduction;
- externally verifiable evidence;
- evidence whose independence is not established.

TA14 Authority will not claim that it has solved the general recursion problem of:

**Who independently attests the attester?**

Where that question remains unresolved, the boundary should be stated rather than concealed.

---

## 39. TA14 Authority Is Subject to Evidentiary Scrutiny

TA14 Authority should be capable of applying to itself the same general principles it asks registrants to respect:

- attribution;
- chronology;
- evidence preservation;
- explicit non-claims;
- versioning;
- provenance;
- limitation disclosure;
- correction;
- dispute preservation;
- independent verification where available;
- explicit identification where independent verification is unavailable.

Institutional authority does not exempt TA14 Authority from evidence discipline.

# PART XIV — INSTITUTIONAL CONTINUITY AND SUCCESSION

## 40. Institutional Continuity Is a Governance Dependency

TA14 Authority recognizes that a registry whose value depends on chronology, attribution, evidence relationships, and durable public records must address what happens if the institution itself becomes unable to continue operating.

Institutional continuity is therefore part of the governance boundary.

---

## 41. Current Succession Status

As of Version 1.1 of these Terms:

**INDEPENDENT SUCCESSOR CUSTODIAN: NOT DESIGNATED**

TA14 Authority will not imply that an independent successor, escrow custodian, archival institution, or continuity operator exists unless one has actually been established.

This status should be updated when a successor or continuity arrangement becomes attributable and effective.

---

## 42. Cessation or Material Loss of Operating Capacity

If TA14 Authority determines that it will cease operation or can no longer reasonably maintain the Registry, the institutional objective should be to preserve the record without manufacturing a false appearance of continuing authority.

Where operationally, legally, and technically possible, TA14 Authority should:

1. stop accepting new governed registrations if continued stewardship cannot be supported;
2. preserve the last authoritative state of active public records;
3. identify the date at which ordinary institutional operations ceased;
4. produce or preserve an export of public registry chronology and public record metadata;
5. preserve available integrity references necessary to evaluate the exported record;
6. distinguish public records from controlled or private evidence;
7. identify whether custody is ending, being archived, or being transferred;
8. identify any successor institution or custodian only after that succession is actually established;
9. preserve provenance showing that the successor did not originate historical TA14 findings it merely inherited;
10. avoid silently rewriting historical records during transfer.

---

## 43. Succession Does Not Create Retrospective Authority

A successor custodian, if one is later established, does not automatically become the author of earlier TA14 findings.

The institutional lineage should distinguish:

**Original TA14 Authority Record**

from

**Successor Custody or Stewardship**

A transfer of custody does not silently transfer authorship, provenance, or historical decision responsibility.

---

## 44. No Guaranteed Perpetual Availability

TA14 Authority does not presently guarantee perpetual technical availability, perpetual hosting, perpetual third-party custody, or indefinite institutional existence.

Where durable continuity mechanisms are not independently established, that limitation must remain visible.

The absence of a presently designated successor is an institutional continuity limitation, not a condition that prevents Governance Entity Registration.

# PART XV — TA14 ERRORS AND INSTITUTIONAL CORRECTION

## 45. TA14 Authority May Be Wrong

A TA14 finding may contain:

- a factual error;
- an evidentiary error;
- an attribution error;
- an interpretation error;
- an incomplete record;
- a methodological limitation.

TA14 Authority does not claim infallibility.

---

## 46. Correction of TA14 Records

Where TA14 Authority identifies a material factual error, it may issue a correction.

The correction should preserve:

- the original record where appropriate;
- the correction date;
- the reason for correction;
- the corrected statement;
- the relationship between the original and corrected records.

TA14 Authority should not silently alter an institutional record in a way that erases the existence of the prior state.

---

## 47. Supersession

Where a later TA14 instrument or finding supersedes an earlier one, the relationship should be explicit.

Possible states include:

- ACTIVE;
- CORRECTED;
- SUPERSEDED;
- RETIRED;
- WITHDRAWN;
- DISPUTED.

Supersession is not silent deletion.

# PART XVII — INDEPENDENT-READ CONTROL

## 50. Independent-Read Validation Is Separate From Publication

Publication of these Terms does not itself establish independent readability.

Independent-read validation is a separate institutional event.

Where no completed independent-read validation record exists, the proper state is:

**INDEPENDENT-READ VALIDATION: NOT YET COMPLETED**

TA14 Authority must not convert publication into a validation claim.

---

## 51. Independent Reader Questions Must Be Preserved Before Explanation

Where an independent-read validation is conducted, material questions raised by the independent reader should be captured in writing before TA14 Authority explains the intended interpretation.

The questions themselves are evidence of where the instrument may depend on unstated context.

The sequence should be:

**Independent Read → Questions Preserved → Divergence Evaluated → Instrument Corrected if Necessary → Re-Test**

not:

**Independent Read → Private Explanation → Reader Re-Briefed → Validation Claimed**

---

## 52. Informed Review and Independent Read Are Different

An informed reader who knows the drafting history may evaluate whether the instrument reflects intended boundaries.

An independent reader evaluates whether the instrument itself carries those boundaries without private context.

Those are different evidentiary roles.

Agreement by an informed participant must not be represented as proof of independent readability.

The purpose of these Terms is not to make every governance claim appear strong.

The purpose is to make the institutional boundary inspectable.

A registrant should be able to understand what registration means.

A third party should be able to understand what it does not mean.

Evidence should remain attributable.

Limitations should remain attached to the conclusions they limit.

Closed systems should not be forced open merely to participate.

Historical records should not be silently rewritten.

A later Terms version should not be allowed to rewrite an earlier event.

The historical fact of an evidence submission should not be confused with an unlimited right or obligation to retain every underlying evidence file forever.

Institutional succession should not be implied where no successor has been established.

Independent verification should be identified where it exists.

Its absence should also be identified.

And TA14 Authority should not ask another governance architecture to live under an evidentiary discipline that TA14 Authority is unwilling to apply to itself.
