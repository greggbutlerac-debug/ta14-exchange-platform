## 1. Purpose

This form is used to test whether a TA14 Authority governance instrument communicates its material meaning without requiring private conversations, drafting history, institutional memory, or explanations from its authors.

This is a review of the **instrument**, not a test of the reviewer.

The governing question is:

> **Can you determine the material rights, boundaries, consequences, and non-claims from the instrument itself?**

---

# PART I — REVIEW CONDITIONS

## 2. Instructions to the Independent Reader

Please review only the instrument identified below.

Do not ask the instrument's authors what they intended before completing this review.

Do not attempt to produce the answer you believe TA14 Authority wants.

Report what the document itself reasonably communicates.

If the answer appears obvious only because you already know something from outside the document, identify that dependency.

If two reasonable interpretations are possible, report both.

If something material is absent, mark it absent.

A missing answer is useful evidence.

# PART II — RESPONSE STATES

## 5. Use One State for Each Question

### CLEAR

The instrument directly provides a reasonably definite answer.

### CLEAR WITH QUALIFICATION

The answer is reasonably clear, but a material qualification is necessary.

### AMBIGUOUS

Two or more materially different reasonable interpretations remain possible.

### IMPLIED ONLY

A likely answer can be inferred, but the instrument does not state it adequately.

### NOT STATED

The instrument does not answer the question.

### INTERNALLY CONFLICTED

Different provisions appear to support materially inconsistent answers.

# Core Rule

> **The reader is not being tested. The instrument is.**

If an informed participant understands a rule but an independent reader reasonably does not, TA14 Authority should examine the rule before relying on private explanation.

The objective is not unanimous interpretation of every sentence.

The objective is to prevent material institutional meaning from depending on conversations that are absent from the governed record.
