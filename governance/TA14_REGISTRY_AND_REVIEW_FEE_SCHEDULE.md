# TA14 Registry & Review Fee Schedule

**Instrument ID:** TA14-FEE-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** PUBLIC FEE DISCLOSURE  
**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026  
**Issued By:** TA14 Authority  
**Related Instrument:** TA14-RET-001 — TA14 Registration & Evidence Terms

---

## 1. Purpose

This schedule identifies the financial boundary between participation in the TA14 AI Governance Exchange and separately scoped professional review or institutional services.

The governing principle is:

> **No undisclosed financial obligation.**

A governance entity should be able to determine whether an activity is free, carries a published fee, or requires separate written scope before committing to that activity.

Registration does not create an obligation to purchase later TA14 services.

---

# PART I — FEE STATES

## 2. Permitted Fee States

Every governed TA14 service covered by this schedule must use one of the following states.

### NO FEE

The activity does not presently carry a TA14 Authority fee.

### PUBLISHED FEE

The activity carries the amount publicly stated in the current version of this schedule.

### SEPARATELY SCOPED

The activity is not automatically included with registration and requires separate written scope and pricing before paid work begins.

### NOT CURRENTLY OFFERED

The activity is not presently available as an institutional service.

A blank fee field is not an authorized fee state.

---

# PART II — GOVERNANCE ENTITY REGISTRATION

## 3. AI Governance Entity Registration

**Service:** Initial AI Governance Entity Registration

**Current Fee State:** NO FEE

**Current Fee:** $0

Registration establishes an attributable governance identity and institutional baseline.

Registration does not constitute:

- certification;
- endorsement;
- validation;
- regulatory approval;
- legal approval;
- technical approval;
- production-readiness determination;
- admissibility finding.

No later paid service is required merely because an entity registers.

---

## 4. Governance Version Registration

**Service:** Registration of a subsequent attributable version of an already registered governance entity

**Current Fee State:** NO FEE

**Current Fee:** $0

A later version remains separately attributable and does not automatically inherit findings from an earlier version.

---

# PART III — ARTIFACT REGISTRATION

## 5. Execution / Evidence Artifact Registration

**Service:** Registration of an eligible execution, evidence, receipt, conformance, demonstration, or related governed artifact

**Current Fee State:** NO FEE

**Current Fee:** $0

Artifact registration requires an eligible registered governance entity.

The governing rule is:

> **No registered governance. No registered artifact.**

Artifact registration preserves identity, attribution, chronology, declared scope, and related registry information.

It does not automatically constitute independent validation of the artifact's substantive claims.

---

# PART IV — GOVERNED DEMONSTRATIONS

## 6. Standard Bounded Demonstration Pathway

**Current Fee State:** NO FEE

**Current Fee:** $0

A standard bounded demonstration may include:

- declared subject;
- frozen version;
- bounded claim;
- explicit non-claims;
- submitted evidence surface;
- evidence admission;
- bounded TA14 finding;
- preserved institutional record.

Availability of a no-fee bounded demonstration does not obligate TA14 Authority to accept an unlimited number of demonstrations, claims, artifacts, or review requests.

TA14 Authority may establish reasonable intake, capacity, scope, and eligibility controls.

---

# PART V — INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE REVIEW

## 7. Extended Independent Evidence Review

**Current Fee State:** SEPARATELY SCOPED

**Current Published Price:** Not applicable — scope required before engagement.

Extended review may include work materially beyond the standard bounded Exchange pathway, including:

- larger evidence packages;
- multiple claims;
- multiple execution routes;
- extended reproduction work;
- comparative evidence analysis;
- route-complete evidence examination;
- additional reviewer coordination;
- specialized technical assessment;
- custom institutional reporting.

No paid extended review begins until scope and price are agreed in writing.

---

# PART VI — DEEPER TECHNICAL AND PRODUCTION-READINESS REVIEW

## 8. Technical / Production-Readiness Review

**Current Fee State:** SEPARATELY SCOPED

**Current Published Price:** Not applicable — scope required before engagement.

This category may include:

- production-readiness assessment;
- route-complete admissibility review;
- implementation-specific review;
- misexecution testing;
- pressure-route examination;
- evidence architecture review;
- operational proof review;
- specialized partner-review layers;
- other substantial professional services.

A registrant is not required to purchase this service.

Registration does not imply that this service will later be necessary.

---

# PART VII — CUSTOM ARCHITECTURE AND ADVISORY WORK

## 9. Architecture Development or Implementation Guidance

**Current Fee State:** SEPARATELY SCOPED

**Current Published Price:** Not applicable — scope required before engagement.

This includes work such as:

- custom architecture design;
- implementation guidance;
- evidence-packet design;
- test-matrix development;
- route definition;
- maturity-roadmap development;
- custom proof-object design;
- bespoke governance engineering;
- substantial architecture consultation.

Such work is outside ordinary registration.

It requires a separate written engagement or another expressly agreed reciprocal arrangement.

---

# PART VIII — PARTNER REVIEW NETWORK

## 10. Multi-Layer Partner Review

**Current Fee State:** SEPARATELY SCOPED

Where a client requests or requires review involving TA14 Authority and one or more independent partner-review layers, the engagement must be separately scoped.

Before work begins, the written scope should identify:

- participating review layers;
- respective responsibilities;
- applicable fees;
- payment allocation where relevant;
- publication boundaries;
- evidence boundaries;
- non-claims;
- independence of participating architectures.

Participation in the TA14 AI Governance Exchange does not automatically enroll a governance entity in a paid Partner Review Network engagement.

---

# PART IX — NO RETROACTIVE FEES

## 11. No Retroactive Charging

TA14 Authority will not retroactively impose a fee on an activity represented as **NO FEE** when the participant completed that activity.

If a future version of this schedule introduces a fee for an activity that was previously free, the new fee applies prospectively according to the effective version of the schedule.

Historical participation remains governed by the fee terms applicable at the relevant time.

---

# PART X — FUTURE PRICE CHANGES

## 12. Versioned Fee Changes

TA14 Authority may change pricing prospectively.

A material pricing change requires:

- an updated version of this schedule;
- a new effective date;
- clear identification of the affected service;
- publication before the new price governs a new commitment.

A fee must not be silently changed inside an already-frozen institutional record.

---

## 13. No Automatic Paid Conversion

A free registration, artifact registration, or bounded demonstration does not automatically convert into a paid subscription, recurring charge, mandatory review plan, or continuing financial obligation.

Any recurring or continuing paid service must be separately disclosed and accepted.

---

# PART XI — THIRD-PARTY COSTS

## 14. External Costs

TA14 Authority fees do not automatically include costs imposed by unrelated third parties.

Examples may include:

- independent laboratories;
- outside technical reviewers;
- legal counsel;
- external auditors;
- testing infrastructure;
- specialist reviewers;
- certification bodies;
- external hosting or reproduction environments.

Where TA14 Authority coordinates a process expected to create a third-party cost for the participant, that cost or pricing mechanism should be disclosed before the participant commits to it.

---

# PART XII — WHAT FREE PARTICIPATION DOES NOT BUY

## 15. No Implied Professional-Service Entitlement

No-fee participation does not create an unlimited entitlement to:

- architecture consulting;
- implementation design;
- remediation guidance;
- custom evidence engineering;
- custom test development;
- unlimited demonstrations;
- unlimited review cycles;
- commercial advisory work;
- bespoke technical support.

TA14 Authority may distinguish between institutional participation and professional services.

---

# PART XIII — WHAT PAID REVIEW DOES NOT BUY

## 16. No Purchased Outcome

Payment for a TA14 review does not purchase:

- a PASS;
- certification;
- endorsement;
- a favorable finding;
- removal of limitations;
- suppression of unfavorable evidence;
- alteration of historical records.

Payment purchases the agreed review work.

The evidence determines the finding.

---

# PART XIV — PUBLIC DISCLOSURE

## 17. Public Fee Legibility

A participant should be able to determine before commitment whether a TA14 pathway is:

**NO FEE**

**PUBLISHED FEE**

**SEPARATELY SCOPED**

or

**NOT CURRENTLY OFFERED**

If that status cannot be determined from the applicable public record, TA14 Authority should clarify the fee boundary before accepting a financial commitment.

---

# Closing Statement

TA14 Authority should not require a governance entity to enter the Exchange without being able to determine the financial consequence of doing so.

Registration may establish identity.

Artifact registration may preserve evidence.

A bounded demonstration may produce an institutional finding.

None of those activities should become an undisclosed mechanism for creating a later financial obligation.

Where deeper professional work is requested, the boundary changes.

That work should be separately scoped, separately priced, and agreed before it begins.

The financial record should be as inspectable as the evidentiary record.
