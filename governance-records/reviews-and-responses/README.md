# TA-14 Reviews & Responses Record

## TA-14 Authority Governance Institution

The TA-14 Reviews & Responses Record preserves attributable responses, reviews,
challenges, corrections, and external publications associated with registered
governance entities, governed demonstrations, execution artifacts, and TA-14
institutional findings.

This record exists to preserve independent voices without collapsing those voices
into TA-14 findings or allowing later commentary to rewrite the original record.

---

## Governing Principle

A governance record should preserve:

Baseline → Demonstration → Evidence → Finding → Response → Challenge → Learning → New Version

Later interpretation must not erase earlier state.

Participant response does not equal TA-14 endorsement.

TA-14 finding does not require participant agreement.

Independent review does not automatically modify an existing finding.

Missing evidence must not be reconstructed retrospectively merely to improve a record.

---

## Record Classes

### 1. Participant Review

A Participant Review is an attributable statement submitted by the steward,
architect, owner, or authorized participant associated with a registered governance
entity or governed demonstration.

Participant Reviews may describe:

- the registration experience;
- baseline preservation;
- version freezing;
- evidence admission;
- demonstration conduct;
- factual-record review;
- disagreement or agreement with findings;
- lessons learned;
- subsequent engineering decisions.

Participant Reviews remain the participant's own voice.

They are not TA-14 findings, certifications, endorsements, or validations.

---

### 2. Participant Response

A Participant Response is an attributable response to a TA-14 finding, artifact,
challenge, or other governed record.

A participant may:

- agree;
- disagree;
- qualify;
- clarify;
- identify limitations;
- provide additional perspective.

A Participant Response does not alter the underlying TA-14 finding unless it
identifies an objective factual error that is processed through the factual
correction pathway.

---

### 3. Independent Review

An Independent Review is submitted by a person or entity that was not responsible
for producing the underlying TA-14 finding.

Independent Reviews may examine:

- evidence sufficiency;
- architecture boundaries;
- authority conditions;
- continuity;
- admissibility;
- binding;
- commit correspondence;
- execution behavior;
- outcome evidence;
- methodology;
- identified limitations.

Independent Reviews must remain attributable to their authors.

TA-14 may publish, respond to, dispute, accept, or decline to adopt conclusions
contained in an Independent Review.

---

### 4. Evidence Challenge

An Evidence Challenge identifies a specific evidentiary issue associated with a
registered claim, governed demonstration, execution artifact, or finding.

A challenge should identify:

- the record being challenged;
- the specific claim or finding at issue;
- the evidentiary basis for the challenge;
- supporting sources or artifacts;
- the identity of the challenger;
- relevant conflicts or affiliations;
- requested disposition, if any.

Challenges should be evidence-based rather than rhetorical.

---

### 5. Factual Correction Request

A Factual Correction Request concerns objective record accuracy.

Examples include:

- incorrect identifier;
- incorrect version;
- incorrect timestamp;
- incorrect hash;
- incorrect artifact description;
- incorrect attribution;
- transcription error.

A factual correction may result in a versioned correction to the affected record.

It does not provide a pathway for negotiating an independent TA-14 determination.

---

### 6. Technical Comment

A Technical Comment provides substantive technical analysis that does not rise to
the level of a formal evidence challenge or independent review.

Technical Comments remain attributable and may be linked to the relevant record.

---

### 7. Replication or Demonstration Request

A person or institution may request that a registered claim, architecture,
behavior, or consequence-bearing route be examined through a future governed
demonstration.

A request does not guarantee acceptance into the demonstration process.

---

### 8. External Publication

External Publications include independently authored:

- articles;
- papers;
- case studies;
- Zenodo records;
- repository publications;
- LinkedIn articles;
- institutional reports;
- research commentary.

External Publications remain under the authorship and responsibility of their
authors.

TA-14 may link them to the relevant governance record without adopting their
conclusions.

---

## Independence Boundary

The TA-14 Authority Governance Institution preserves separate institutional voices.

TA-14 publishes what admitted evidence supports.

Participants may publish what they learned or how they interpret the process.

Independent reviewers may challenge either.

The public should be able to inspect each record without those voices being merged
into a negotiated narrative.

---

## Chronology Preservation

Earlier records must remain accessible when later records are created.

Where a review, challenge, demonstration, or finding produces later engineering
changes, the preferred sequence is:

Original Registered Baseline
→ Governed Demonstration
→ Evidence Admission
→ Independent Finding
→ Participant Response
→ Engineering Learning
→ New Version
→ New Registered Baseline
→ Future Demonstration

The prior record should not be rewritten to make later improvement appear to have
always existed.

---

## Public Submission Direction

The TA-14 AI Governance Exchange is intended to support future governed submission
pathways for:

- Participant Reviews
- Participant Responses
- Independent Reviews
- Evidence Challenges
- Factual Correction Requests
- Technical Comments
- Replication Requests
- External Publication References

Submitted material should be attributable, dated, linked to a specific governed
record, and preserved with clear authorship and boundary conditions.
