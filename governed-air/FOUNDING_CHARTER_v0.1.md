# GOVERNED AIR — Founding Charter v0.1

Status: PRIVATE DEVELOPMENT DRAFT — not approved, registered, validated, or publicly launched.
Institutional sponsor: TA14 Authority Governance Institution.

## Mission
Connect environmental observation to admissible evidence, applicable authority, established standing, authorized intervention, and verified outcomes. Governed Air is an application of TA14's existing architecture, not a new regulator or an independent authority over participants.

## Governing question
Does this proposed consequence have sufficient Admissible Evidence, Applicable Authority, and Established Standing to become reality NOW?

## Governing sequence
Reality → Record → Continuity → Admissibility → Binding → Commit → Execution → Outcome.
Dispositions: ALLOW / HOLD / DENY / ESCALATE, always bounded by scope, evidence, actor, and authority. A disposition in a simulation has no real-world execution authority.

## Initial demonstrations
1. Indoor classroom CO2 concern: sensor provenance, calibration, temporal continuity, applicable policy, authorized investigation, follow-up measurement. Elevated CO2 alone is not a diagnosis or automatic instruction to change equipment.
2. Outdoor PM2.5 policy-action case: provenance, exposure interpretation, jurisdiction, accountable policy actor, decision record, outcome verification.
All readings, actors, policies, and outcomes in the initial demonstrations are hypothetical and clearly labeled.

## Participation
Public education and simulated exploration are open. Technical critique and contribution are attributed only with contributor consent. Formal examination, governance registration, and deployment require separately established scope and terms. No organizational affiliation, approval, certification, or endorsement is implied by outreach or citation.

## Boundaries
No autonomous physical actuation in the initial release. No invented calibration, legal authority, clinical conclusion, regulatory compliance, or field outcome. Safety-critical situations follow independently applicable emergency procedures. Preserve raw observations separately from derived or adjusted values. Protect personal and location data; collect only necessary information.

## Technical deliverables
- Public-facing educational showroom after review.
- Private deterministic decision demonstrator with replayable test fixtures and reasoned dispositions.
- Evidence schema: source, instrument, measurement, timestamp, provenance, continuity, uncertainty, policy reference, actor, capacity, standing basis, authority relationship, proposed action, disposition, verification.
- Exportable human-readable examination record and machine-readable audit record.
- Explicit tests for missing evidence, missing standing, stale readings, conflicts, and escalation.

## Quality gates before launch
Scientific review; authority and standing review; technical reproducibility; accessibility; privacy/security; honest demonstration labeling; explicit consent for named partners and logos. No public deployment before gates pass.

## Commercial boundaries
Public teaching is distinct from paid institutional examination, integrations, licensing, and deployment. Commercial claims require evidence and contractual terms. No guarantee of external participation, fundraising, or advertising outcomes.
